    How it works at the tattoo shop

    Which file to give the artist

    1. Design PDF and Artist sheet PDF.
    2. Print at Actual size (100%), not "Fit to page". To check the printer, print page 2 of the artist sheet the same way: its 6-inch bar must measure 6 inches.
    3. Design PNG if the artist wants a picture file. Design SVG only if they ask for a vector file.
    4. The files are not mirrored. Do not flip them; the artist takes care of the stencil.

    What happens at the shop

    1. Consult. Bring the artist sheet and a few pictures from this page. Size, placement, line thickness and which way the writing reads are settled with the artist.
    2. Stencil. The artist makes a stencil from the file, with a thermal stencil printer and stencil paper, or by tracing it by hand.
    3. Placement. The stencil is pressed onto the skin and leaves an outline. It can be wiped off and placed again until it is right. Look at it directly on the arm; a mirror shows writing backwards.
    4. Tattoo. The artist follows the outline.
    5. Aftercare. Follow the artist's instructions.

    Pictures from this page are previews. A flat photo cannot show how the design wraps around the arm.
